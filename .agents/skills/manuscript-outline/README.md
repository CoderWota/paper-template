# Manuscript Outline

Plans the paper's argument and sections with you: the main claim, the supporting claims in order with the evidence each needs, what each section does, the planned figures and tables, the venue's limits, and what is still missing.

Ask, for example: "Help me structure the paper around my research question and the results we have."

## What to expect

- Claims without support appear as gaps, not as findings.
- No fixed article structure is imposed; the structure follows your discipline, article type, and target venue.
- You confirm the structure before it becomes the plan in `manuscript/outline.md`. The outline guides writing and is never part of the assembled manuscript.
- The agent also creates `manuscript/logic-map.md`: a Mermaid flowchart for the whole-paper argument and local section reasoning. It makes evidence gaps and limits visible and is never part of the assembled manuscript. The [logic map guide](../../../docs/logic-map-guide.md) explains its status labels and review checks.

The agent instructions are in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Related skills: `manuscript-writing`, `research-planning`, `journal-selection`.
