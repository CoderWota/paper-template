# Manuscript Outline

Plans the paper's argument and sections with you: premises, evidence, original author analyses and inferences, intermediate judgments, and the main conclusion. It connects that argument to sections, figures, tables, venue limits, and open issues.

Ask, for example: "Help me structure the paper around my research question and the results we have."

## What to expect

- Claims without support appear as gaps, not as findings.
- No fixed article structure is imposed; the structure follows your discipline, article type, and target venue.
- You confirm the structure before it becomes the plan in `manuscript/outline.md`. The outline guides writing and is never part of the assembled manuscript.
- The agent creates `manuscript/logic-map.md` with a Mermaid argument graph and reasoning register, then maintains it automatically with each writing task. Saved input versions expose a stale map after direct edits. The [logic map guide](../../../docs/logic-map-guide.md) explains the support routes, inference checks, and synchronization loop. Internal files stay outside publication output.

The agent instructions are in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Related skills: `manuscript-writing`, `research-planning`, `journal-selection`.
