# Writing Requirements

Active, researcher-confirmed writing rules and terminology for the manuscript. Each rule states its scope (the whole manuscript, a section, or an output profile) and, where helpful, an example or an allowed exception.

## Researcher control of manuscript tone

- Rule: Follow the researcher's confirmed tone and style preferences for each scope. Communication rules and general style advice do not override them. Do not impose short sentences, common words, or ASD-STE100 on manuscript prose, and do not infer tone preferences.
- Scope: Drafting, revision, and translation of the manuscript. The researcher confirms the scope of each tone preference.
- Confirmed: 2026-10-30; "We also need to consider the tone of AI-written papers. It must follow the researcher's preferences and must not be overridden by absolute rules."
