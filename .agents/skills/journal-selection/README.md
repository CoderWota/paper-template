# Journal Selection

Compares journals or other venues for your paper, or examines one venue in detail, from its current official pages: scope, article types, length and structure, AI-use policy, data and reporting policies, costs, and relevant recent publications.

Ask, for example: "Compare these three journals for my paper," or "Does this journal allow AI-assisted drafting?"

## What to expect

- Every requirement comes with its source address and the date it was read; anything not checked is labeled as such.
- Fit is assessed separately from the paper's impact and readiness. Journal selection itself gives no paper-specific odds; an explicit handoff to `mock-review` follows that skill's separate simulated outcome-distribution rule.
- The notes are kept in `research/venues/`. Choosing the target venue is your decision; once you confirm it, it is recorded in `requirements/project.md`.

The agent instructions are in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Related skills: `impact-assessment`, `submission-assembly`, `submission-readiness`.
