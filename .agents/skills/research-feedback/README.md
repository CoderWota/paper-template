# Research Feedback

Organizes advice from co-researchers, collaborators, coauthors, supervisors, or anyone else, whatever their title: commented documents, a suggested outline, meeting notes, or what someone told you. Each event is kept in `research/feedback/<feedback-id>/` with the received material, a readable copy, and a list of suggestions.

Ask, for example: "Organize these comments from my collaborators, discuss their implications with me, and apply the changes I choose."

## What to expect

- Each suggestion keeps its own author and location, even in a document or meeting with several people. Exact words, your recollections, and the agent's summaries are labeled differently.
- Advice never becomes a rule or a change by itself. The agent compares it with the evidence and your requirements, shows conflicts, and asks you to decide; suggestions are not ranked by seniority.
- A suggestion is marked implemented only when the actual change exists.

The agent instructions are in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Related skills: `constraint-maintenance`, `review-ingestion` (for journal reviews), `source-ingestion`.
