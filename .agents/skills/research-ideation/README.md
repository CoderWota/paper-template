# Research Ideation

Helps you turn an interest, a puzzle, readings, or data into a few research directions that differ in kind, such as a new observation, an explanation, a method, a dataset, or an interpretation. Each direction states its question, possible contribution, basis, main assumption, feasibility, and the first check worth making.

Ask, for example: "Based on these readings and my interest in urban heat, suggest several distinct research directions."

## What to expect

- Directions are grounded in your material and your words; nothing is presented as new or unprecedented, and no literature gap is invented.
- Only the directions you ask to keep are saved, as notes in `ideas/`.
- A kept idea is not a rule, a decision, or a finding.

The agent instructions are in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Related skills: `idea-deepening`, `impact-assessment`, `literature-discovery`, `research-planning`.
