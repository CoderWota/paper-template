# Manuscript Writing

Drafts or revises manuscript text on request: a new section, a rewritten paragraph, a shortened abstract, a translation of your draft into the target paper language, a results passage, a caption, or a single sentence. Your notes and working drafts may use any language; the agent preserves that language for an ordinary edit. Text prepared for publication and its figure and table captions use your chosen paper language. The agent follows your confirmed writing rules and relies on actual evidence and results.

Ask, for example: "Draft the methods section from the plan and the results," "Translate my Chinese notes for the introduction into English," or "Shorten this paragraph."

## What to expect

- Empirical claims use checked sources/results or an explicit preliminary or missing status. Original analyses, deductions, interpretations, and judgments use explicit premises, reasoning, conditions, and criteria.
- Each manuscript write includes semantic synchronization of the internal argument graph and reasoning register. The agent records the compared input version after inspecting the change; the graph can stay unchanged for a spelling edit. Project checks reveal later direct edits. The internal files stay outside publication output.
- Formulas, figures, tables, citations, and cross-references follow the workspace conventions, so they render on GitHub and export correctly later.
- A polish changes only what needs it, and says so when nothing does. After a translation or a rewrite, a script compares the new text with the original so that no number, citation, or condition is lost, and, when your manuscript is in another language, you receive a literal rendering in your own language to check the meaning.
- Your own edits are kept. A one-time request does not become a rule.
- When a major revision supplies a staged candidate, the agent edits and checks that candidate with the project's requirements and bibliography, then returns it to the revision coordinator for application.
