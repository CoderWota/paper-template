# Research Records

Records of how the research is directed and what it has produced: the plan, consequential choices you have confirmed, attributed advice from other people, venue assessments, and the project's own results with their provenance. They preserve rationale and history so that later work does not have to reconstruct them.

## Contents

- [`decisions/`](decisions/README.md): research decision records, stating what you chose, its scope, and why.
- [`feedback/`](feedback/README.md): research feedback, stating what co-researchers, collaborators, coauthors, supervisors, or other contributors advised, and what happened to the advice.
- [`results/`](results/README.md): result records for the project's own results: inputs, method or code version, outputs, checks, limitations, and the claims that use them.
- [`venues/`](venues/README.md): assessments of journals or other venues, read from their current official sources.
- `plan.md`: the research plan, created when you ask for one: claims and the evidence they need, methods or lines of argument, resources, work items, risks, and the next action. Parts that you have not confirmed stay marked as proposed.
- `impact.md`: impact assessments that you asked to keep: why a contribution matters, to whom, what supports and limits it, and how to strengthen it. An assessment is not evidence.

## How these differ from requirements

Feedback records what was advised. Decisions record what you chose and why. [`requirements/`](../requirements/README.md) states what currently governs the work. Receiving advice is not adopting it, and adopting a change is not implementing it. A plan states proposed work; a result record states what was actually done and found. Agents read these records when a task needs them, not in every session.

The records are written in English. The labels in each folder's README are suggested layouts. `python scripts/research.py check` follows recognized fields when it checks links and provenance; unfamiliar labels or status words are left uninterpreted, not rejected.

Formal editor and reviewer correspondence is kept separately in [`reviews/`](../reviews/README.md).
