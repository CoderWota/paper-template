# Natural Prose

Revises text that reads as machine-written, formulaic, inflated, or translated. The revised text keeps every claim and reads like the work of a careful human author. It works on manuscript passages, responses to reviewers, and other text you give it, in English, Chinese, Japanese, and other languages. It can also only check a text and list what it finds.

Ask, for example: "This paragraph sounds as if an AI wrote it. Make it read naturally without changing the content." Or: "Check the introduction for formulaic wording, but do not change anything yet."

## What to expect

- Only passages with a real problem change; sound sentences stay as they are.
- Every claim, number, citation, cross-reference, hedge, and condition stays, and nothing is added. A script compares the revised text with the original, and every difference is explained to you.
- Your confirmed writing rules and your own style come first. A habit that the lists would flag stays when it is yours.
- Persuasive impact language and ambitious prospects can stay. Strong wording alone is not a factual error; the agent flags a specific unsupported assertion rather than automatically weakening your contribution.
- The agent does not judge whether a text was written by AI and does not promise that a text will pass an AI detector. Editing the wording does not change what a venue requires you to disclose about AI assistance.
