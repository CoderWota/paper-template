# Manuscript Writing

Drafts or revises manuscript text on request: a new section, a rewritten paragraph, a shortened abstract, or a single sentence. The agent writes in your manuscript language, follows your confirmed writing rules and the outline, and relies only on actual evidence and results.

Ask, for example: "Draft the methods section from the plan and the results," or "Shorten this paragraph."

## What to expect

- Claims are supported by sources whose support was checked or by checked results; anything without support is marked `[TODO: ...]` in the text instead of being invented.
- Formulas, figures, tables, citations, and cross-references follow the workspace conventions, so they render on GitHub and export correctly later.
- Your own edits are kept. A one-time request does not become a rule.

The agent instructions are in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Related skills: `manuscript-audit`, `manuscript-revision`, `literature-and-evidence`, `constraint-maintenance`.
