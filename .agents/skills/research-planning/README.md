# Research Planning

Turns a selected research question into a feasible plan: the claims and the evidence each needs, methods or lines of argument suited to your discipline, the resources you have and lack, ordered work items with owners, risks, and the next action.

Ask, for example: "Make a research plan for this question, and show which evidence we already have and which work is still needed."

## What to expect

- The plan separates evidence already available from work still required, and never writes an expected finding as a result.
- No experiment or article structure is forced on research that does not need it.
- The plan proposes; you choose. Method and scope choices are recorded only after you confirm them. The plan is kept in `research/plan.md`.

The agent instructions are in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). Related skills: `research-code-setup`, `research-investigation`, `literature-discovery`.
