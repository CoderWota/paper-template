# Outline

The argument and section plan for the manuscript, with empirical, analytical, and decision gaps noted. This file guides writing; it is never assembled into the manuscript. Its companion [logic map and reasoning register](logic-map.md) records the author's premises, analyses, inferences, judgments, conditions, and conclusions at whole-paper and local levels. The framework is synchronized with each writing task and stays outside publication output.
