# Research Records

Records of how the research is directed: consequential choices the researcher has confirmed, and attributed advice from other people. They preserve rationale and collaboration history so that later work does not have to reconstruct them.

## Contents

- [`decisions/`](decisions/README.md): research decision records, stating what you chose, its scope, and why.
- [`feedback/`](feedback/README.md): research feedback, stating what co-researchers, collaborators, coauthors, supervisors, or other contributors advised, and what happened to the advice.

## How these differ from requirements

Feedback records what was advised. Decisions record what you chose and why. [`requirements/`](../requirements/README.md) states what currently governs the work. Receiving advice is not adopting it, and adopting a change is not implementing it. Agents read these records when a task needs them, not in every session.

Formal editor and reviewer correspondence is kept separately in [`reviews/`](../reviews/README.md).
