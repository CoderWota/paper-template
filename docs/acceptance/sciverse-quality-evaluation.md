# Literature Search and Evidence Quality Evaluation

This is a small development evaluation, not a complete reference set or evidence of service quality. It contains only public identifiers and brief original descriptions, not paper text. The cases cover English and Chinese queries across computer science, climate science and clinical research; the cited source texts are in English. They do not establish performance on Chinese-language papers. Source landing pages or abstracts were inspected; full papers and precise passages have not yet been independently checked for this evaluation. Complete those checks before treating the expected findings as a gold standard.

## Evaluation procedure

1. Before sending any research question or identifiers externally, use the confirmed provider scope. A public evaluation query still consumes account quota; estimate the maximum requests and confirm any charge before a run. Use one page per query initially and continue only to answer a defined gap. An initial filtered search uses one catalog request and one search request; resumed pages reuse the recorded catalog snapshot and each require a search request. A relation page and each content read are additional requests. Count all calls, including failures and retries, and record access or cost uncertainty. Do not run Paper Schema or resources unless their individual operations are being evaluated.
2. Record the date, exact query, provider, parameters, account access limitations, page count, returned count, failures, and the number of API calls. Preserve each result's identity and version; record whether the known lead was returned within the inspected results. Distinguish a missing result from a query or service failure, and record which full texts were accessible.
3. For each candidate answer, a reviewer checks each substantive claim against its cited source and actual locator. Mark direct support, contradiction, qualification, no support, or not checked. Record source version, population/material, method, outcome, source-access level and locator. A snippet or abstract cannot establish that the full article was inspected. Review both evidence favorable to an answer and materially different or contrary findings.
4. Report, per case, whether the known lead was found, number of inspected hits, verified claims over claims needing verification, invalid/missing locators, unsupported claims, overlooked counterevidence, and inaccessible sources. State denominators and unresolved items. Do not combine these small cases into a representative recall estimate or claim exhaustive coverage.
5. Store run observations outside this template's tracked fixtures, or in an explicitly requested development report containing no researcher material. Record the date and test environment. Do not mark an outcome as passed unless a reviewer actually inspected the relevant evidence.

## Current status

On 2026-10-28, a bounded retrieval smoke run made four `agentic-search` requests, one per question, with `top_k=5` and no retries or follow-up calls. Each returned five records. The known climate-paper title appeared in the top five; the expected Transformer paper and the two named clinical trials were not identified in the top five by title or their listed identifiers. No content was fetched, and no result was checked against a full-text original. This is a small discovery observation, not a completed quality evaluation or a general recall estimate. Account-specific billing, query retention, and training use remain unknown. Individual Paper Schema and resource operations have synthetic tests only; multilingual coverage, contrary-evidence retrieval, and original-checked citation accuracy remain unverified.
